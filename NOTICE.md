HUDKit is MIT licensed (see [LICENSE](LICENSE)). It carries one port from another MIT project:

## SpeakFree

`Sources/HUDKit/UI/HUDNotchGeometry.swift` ports the frame math of SpeakFree's
`OverlayScreenGeometry` and `OverlayLayout` (`Sources/SpeakFreeLib/OverlayPlacement.swift`),
generalized here for a panel of any size rather than only the recording indicator's pill.
